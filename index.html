Tee Box Bookie

Privacy Policy

Tee Box Bookie ("we", "our", or "us") is a golf scorekeeping application built for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • First and last name (required) — used to identify you to other players in shared rounds and friends features.
  • Email address (required) — used to create your account and allow other users to find and add you as a friend.
  • Phone number (optional) — stored on your profile if you choose to provide it.
  • Handicap index (optional) — used to calculate net scores during rounds.
  • Profile photo (optional) — displayed to friends and other players in shared rounds.

During app use, we also store:

  • Golf round data — scores, courses played, dates, and betting game results.
  • Friend relationships — the list of users you have added as friends.

2. Location Data

Tee Box Bookie requests access to your device's location only while the app is in use, and only when you are actively playing a round. Location is used exclusively to calculate live yardages to the green and hazards on the current hole.

Your location is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere. We do not collect, log, or share your GPS coordinates.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your profile to friends and players in shared rounds.
  • To calculate scores, handicaps, and betting game results.
  • To allow friends to see your completed rounds and live rounds in progress.
  • To generate personal statistics and your TBB Index (app-estimated handicap).

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics platforms, or any purpose unrelated to the app's golf scorekeeping functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

All account and round data is stored using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure, which is subject to Apple's security standards and privacy policies. Data syncs between your devices via your iCloud account. Local data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework.

We do not operate our own servers. We do not have access to your Apple ID credentials.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is shared only in the following limited ways:

  • With friends you add — your name, profile photo, and round results are visible to users you have accepted as friends.
  • Live rounds — if you share a round code with others, they can view your live scores as a spectator. You control who receives the code.
  • Apple / CloudKit — data is stored and transmitted via Apple's CloudKit platform per Apple's privacy policy.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can manage and delete your data at any time from within the app:

  • Delete all rounds — Profile → Settings → Data Management → Delete All Rounds.
  • Delete your account — Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, all rounds, and all associated data.

Because all data is stored in CloudKit, deleting your account removes it from Apple's servers. There is no separate request required.

7. Children's Privacy

Tee Box Bookie is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
